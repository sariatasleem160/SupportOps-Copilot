# Acme SaaS Support Policy

**Effective date:** January 2026  
**Applies to:** All customer-facing support agents and AI-assisted draft replies

## 1. Tone and professionalism

- Acknowledge the customer's concern within the first sentence.
- Use empathetic, neutral language. Never blame the customer.
- Match formality to the situation; stay professional even when the customer is upset.

## 2. Refunds and billing

- **Standard refund window:** 30 days from purchase for annual plans; 14 days for monthly plans.
- Do **not** promise an immediate refund in the first reply. Say: *"I will review your account and follow up within one business day."*
- For duplicate charges, confirm the billing email and last four digits of the payment method before crediting.
- Escalate refund requests over **$500 USD** to the billing lead.

## 3. Account access

- Never send passwords or temporary credentials in email.
- Direct customers to the self-service password reset link.
- For locked accounts after failed login attempts, explain the 30-minute lockout policy.
- Verify identity using the email on file; do not repeat full email addresses in replies.

## 4. Technical issues

- Ask for browser/OS version and steps to reproduce when not already provided.
- Do not disclose internal stack traces, server names, or unreleased roadmap details.
- Offer known workarounds from the public knowledge base when available.

## 5. Shipping (physical add-ons)

- Standard shipping: 5–7 business days domestic, 10–14 international.
- For lost packages, open a carrier trace after 7 days domestic / 14 days international.
- Never guarantee delivery dates; provide tracking links when available.

## 6. Feature requests

- Thank the customer for feedback.
- Do not commit to delivery dates.
- Log the request and mention it will be shared with the product team.

## 7. Privacy and PII

- Do not echo full email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, or payment card numbers in replies.
- Refer to *"the email on your account"* or *"your order on file"* instead.
- All ticket logs must use redacted customer text.

## 8. Escalation

- **Urgent priority** or **SLA risk** tickets must be flagged for human review within 15 minutes.
- Legal threats, data-breach reports, and VIP accounts require immediate supervisor escalation.

## 9. Reply length

- Keep first replies under **150 words** unless troubleshooting steps require more detail.
